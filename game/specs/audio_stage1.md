# Willow native engine: remaining stage-1 sound commands

Date 2026-11-02. Extends `audio.md` (pipeline, section 6) and the first
audio slice (0x13, 0x20, 0x2A, 0x2B, 0x31, 0x43, 0x44). Tags as in audio.md:
VERIFIED = checked today in this repo, SOURCE = read from the sound program
or other specs, UNKNOWN = not established.

Approved approximations used here (user): music note onsets rounded to 60 Hz
frames; FM sound effects played as PCM rendered from the original chip.
Instruments may be adapted.

All files are regenerated by `tools/audio/run.sh` (headless, ~30 s).

## 1. What each command is

Z80 dispatch (SOURCE, disassembly of `soundcpu.bin`): 00-1F fixed table
(music, 8-track loader 01FA), 20-44 bank-1 table (single-stream YM effect
loader 0230; header bit 6 picks effect slot D540 / voice 6, else D500 /
voice 7; low nibble = priority, new >= current wins), 81-EF OKI sample
`(cmd & 7F) mod 42` with voice/attenuation byte 0E43[id], F0-FF system
(table 02EE).

All five music commands are canonical fixed-table music cues (header 08,
6 tracks). None of the effects writes OKI ports (VERIFIED: 0 OKI writes in
their logs).

### 0x01 and the 0x01FA word (SOURCE + VERIFIED)
"Music word 1" is the command 0x01 (F00A parameter 0). The following word
0x01FA is system command **FA** with parameter 1 (high byte -> F00A, low
byte -> F008, PORTING.md 23116). FA (handler 03AF): if no fade is running,
music attenuation D010 := 0x7F (silent), then D010 decreases by 1 every
F00A ticks until it passes 0: a **fade-in** from silence over 127 x 1 ticks
= 0.51 s. Queue order (783C/77EA: one word per main-CPU IRQ, `00FF`
after each command) puts FA two 60 Hz IRQs = 8 service ticks after 0x01.
The log is made with `--then 0xFA@8:1`, so the fade-in is in the data:
the first 8 ticks at full level, then silence fading in (headless RMS 21
over frames 34-64, 5,592 later; VERIFIED). The game therefore ignores
0x01FA itself (`sound_post` switches on the low byte; FA is a no-op).

### Loops and ends (VERIFIED)
- 0x10/0x12/0x14/0x15: `opmlog` reports a 256-tick "loop" after the last
  key-on; in that part only the timer registers (10-14) are written and only
  the tick counter D014 changes. The tracks have stopped: converted as
  one-shots (`opm2opn.py` now detects this), plus 0.3 s of release (the
  ymfm render falls below -50 dB within 0.1 s after the end). XGM2 then
  reports not playing; the game must post the next music itself.
- 0x01: the whole sound RAM never repeats within 65,536 ticks. Per-track
  states (D100 + 0x30 n): tracks 0, 1, 3 repeat with period 9,280 ticks from
  tick 987, track 4 from 1030, track 2 with period 116, track 5 (voice 5,
  a percussion-like pattern, KC 08/0D/3A/50 on a 58/116-tick grid) with its
  own period **5,336** ticks. The exact repeat would be lcm = 213,440 ticks
  (855 s). The VGM loops ticks 1030..10309 (9,280 ticks, 37.17 s; D014 only
  matters by parity, bit 0 at 0509, and 9,280 is even). **Approximation:**
  track 5's pattern restarts its phase at each loop instead of drifting
  against the other tracks (its beat grid stays aligned).

## 2. Conversion

- Music: `opmlog` (arcade Z80 program, headless) -> `opm2opn.py` default plan
  (voices 0-4 FM 1-5, 5-7 PSG) -> VGM -> XGM2 by rescomp. New options:
  `--loop TICK` (forced loop), `--lead FRAMES` (chip set-up: converter reset
  plus the program's tick-0 voice reset, no key-on, one frame before the
  first notes; all five new songs use 1, a 16.7 ms later start, because
  otherwise the first frame is over XGM2's frame size: xgm2tool warned for
  0x01/0x12 and failed for 0x15). music13 is unchanged (still one xgm2tool
  frame-size warning, as before).
- Note onsets moved to 60 Hz frames: max 8.3 ms, mean 3.3-4.1 ms per song.
  Same-frame key-off/on pairs merged: 174 (0x01), 1-13 (jingles).
  OPM LFO under 2 Hz switched off (0x01: 717 writes), PSG volumes clamped
  (215 in 0x01); no DT2, no F-number clamp.
- Headless check of the converted VGMs against the ymfm render of the
  original logs: RMS per second within ~10 % (e.g. 0x01 5,323 / 5,553,
  0x14 4,940 / 4,896).
- FM effects: ymfm YM2151 render of the original register writes
  (`opmrender --tail 3`, all end with key-offs, the release is kept), then
  `wavtrim.py`: cut after the last sample >= 192 (16-bit; below half an
  8-bit step) with a 3 ms fade. 44.1 kHz WAV; rescomp resamples to 13.3 kHz.
- OKI voices: `okiwav.py` imports the decoder and resampler of
  `tools/oki_samples.py` (MAME ADPCM tables; that script itself is not run
  because it rewrites build/ files), 1 MHz/132 = 7,575.76 Hz source,
  Blackman-sinc to **13,300 Hz** (XGM2 full rate, no halfRate), attenuation
  from 0E43 (all 0 = full level), trimmed the same way. Lengths equal the
  source lengths (no trailing silence in the samples).

ROM cost of the new resources (rescomp output, 256-byte aligned, from
`res/audio.h`): 201,472 B of PCM (FM effects 86,016, OKI voices 115,456)
and 6,912 B of music (XGC packed); all audio together 314,368 B. Playing the OKI voices at 6,650 Hz (halfRate) would save ~57 KB at
the cost of 3.3-3.8 kHz content.

## 3. Game side (`src/sound.c`, `src/sound.h`, `res/audio.res`)

`sound_post(word)` switches on the low byte (the Z80 command).
Music: `XGM2_play` of `music_boss` (0x01), `music_clear` (0x10),
`music_map` (0x12), `music_stage1` (0x13), `music_over` (0x14),
`music_continue` (0x15). 0xF0 (Z80 030E: effect reset + music voices off,
saved music cleared) -> `XGM2_stop` + the 0xF4 action. FA/FB and other
commands are ignored.

Effects use a per-channel model of the arcade effect slot: a new effect
replaces the current one unless the current one has a higher priority and is
still inside its hold time; after the hold, anything may cut the remaining
release (as a new arcade effect takes over the YM voice). The driver
priority passed to `XGM2_playPCMEx` is a constant 8, so XGM2 never refuses.

| Ch | Effect (priority, hold frames) |
|---|---|
| 1 | shot 0x20 (4, 26), splash 0x38 (4, 27), countdown 0x42 (4, 33), hurt 0x82 (12, whole), death 0x83 (14, whole) |
| 2 | landing 0x2B (2, 8), slide 0x2A (2, 17), text 0x2F (2, 7), count 0x30 (2, 5), time warning 0x43 (3, 16), arrow 0x34 (4, 23), pickup 0x2D (5, 14), enemy hit 0x32 (6, 14), explosion 0x33 (7, 36) |
| 3 | charge 0x31 (3, whole), charged 0x44 (3, 85), roar 0x8A (8, whole), fire breath 0x8D (8, whole), sorcerer 0x8C (10, whole), boss killed 0x8E (10, whole) |

Holds: arcade effect length (log end tick x 60 / 249.7) for FM effects,
whole sample for voices; the explosion holds 36 frames (its loud part, to
-20 dB) so frequent hits/pickups cannot cut it. 0xF4 stops channel 3 only
when a charge sound owns it (fire breath, roar and boss voices survive it, as
OKI voices survive F4 on the arcade).

Deviations from the arcade's own priorities (deliberate, user request:
important cues must not be cut by frequent ones): explosion 7 > hit 6 >
pickup 5 on channel 2 (arcade: pickup 9, hit 10, explosion 10, so a hit
would cut an explosion); text/count ticks at 2. Channels are 3 instead of
2 FM slots + 4 OKI voices: shots are refused while hurt/death play;
charge sounds are refused while a boss voice plays; arcade OKI voice 2
(hurt, death, roar, fire) is split over channels 1 and 3, so a hurt no
longer cuts a fire breath.

Headless check (VERIFIED, `-DSNDTEST -DSNDSEQ` build: a schedule in
`sound_debug` posts every cue; channel masks read with PEEK, audio with
AUDIO=): every cue plays on its channel for its length; death holds
channel 1 against a shot; explosion refuses a hit 5 frames later and
accepts one 50 frames later; fire breath survives 0xF4, the charge sound
does not; boss music keeps playing through its loop point (frame 2508,
RMS 6,017 before / 5,651 after); jingles stop at their ends (XGM2 not
playing, output 0); 0xF0 silences. Normal build: boots, stage music RMS
2,500-5,600 from the second second (400 frames).

## 4. Open questions

1. Music 0x13 (stage 1) is posted with 0x02FA: a 1.02 s fade-in from
   silence after ~8 ticks, not in the current music13 data (made without
   `--then`). Regenerating it with `--then 0xFA@8:2` would match the arcade;
   not done here (music13 was checked against the golden fixtures).
2. Stage start also posts 0x0000 + 0x01FA (checkpoint music word 0) before
   0x0013 + 0x02FA. Command 0x00 is music cue 0 (6 tracks); whether it is
   audible before 0x13 replaces it (same frame or one later) is UNKNOWN; not
   converted.
3. Arcade music priority and save/restore (01B8 saves the current music to
   D300 when a cue of another priority starts; F2 = 0314 restores it) are not
   modelled: XGM2 plays only the latest song.
4. 0x08FB / 0x10FB (FB: mix ramp-up, then F4) are ignored; F7 (OKI stop
   0x18) is not mapped (it would stop voice-channel effects).
5. Listening test by the user: 60 Hz quantization in the boss music (174
   merged retriggers) and the track-5 phase reset at each 37 s loop.
